What we know

Motion: the settle

  1. Motion follows sound. The layer rises as the sound fades in, then holds completely still.
  2. One curve, no bounce. 1.4 s ease-out up, 2 s down. Nothing springs or pulses.
  3. Never draw the beat. No pulsing at 14 Hz or anywhere near it.
  4. Reduce Motion means a plain fade, and sound still eases in over 8 s.
