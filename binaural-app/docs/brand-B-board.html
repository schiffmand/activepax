The idea in one line

Your audio on top, the light underneath. The flame is split blue and saffron because binaural beats need two ears with two different tones. When the headphone check hears both sides, the halves meet into one flame.

The flame's shape tells you the mode: tall for Focus, round for Calm, leaning for Unwind, a low ember for Sleep, perfectly still for Steady.

Tealight is a working name for brand direction B. It isn't a medical device or a treatment for any condition. The evidence for binaural beats is mixed, and the brand says so.
